# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to **contact@token-riot.com**, the project's
sole contact address. Include the version or commit, impact and minimal steps
to reproduce the issue. Do not include credentials, customer data or unnecessary
personal information. Do not post secrets or sensitive exploits in public issues.

The game's Node server is designed for local loopback use. Tokens are virtual;
local storage is not an authority for real payments. The proprietary store and
its payment service are separate projects.

Release files undergo pattern scanning and automated checks. Passing checks
does not guarantee the absence of vulnerabilities. Private development history
and internal audit records are not part of the public release.
